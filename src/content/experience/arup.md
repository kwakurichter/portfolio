## Overview

My time at Arup was short but dense. I worked with the mechanical team in the Toronto office on fast-paced building design projects that required coordination across disciplines.

## Projects

### UofT Lash Miller

Built heating and cooling plant schematics and modeled plumbing lab services for the University of Toronto Lash Miller Chemistry Building Expansion. This supported an on-time Detailed Design package for costing.

### TR6

Coordinated with electrical and structural teams and the client to analyze routing options for a chilled-water loop serving a new 2 MW data center while limiting cost and meeting project specifications.

### UofT Rotman

Collaborated with the civil team to draft a proposal for a new underground trench and chamber service connection for the University of Toronto Rotman building, reducing spatial requirements by approximately 20%.

### TMU Indigenous Student Centre

Conducted ventilation and plumbing calculations for the Indigenous Student Centre renovation at Toronto Metropolitan University, using ASHRAE guidelines to support VAV sizing for the 50% Detailed Design submission.
