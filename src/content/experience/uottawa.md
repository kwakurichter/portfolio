## Research

I am a Master of Applied Science candidate in Mechanical Engineering at the University of Ottawa and a member of the D06 Robotics Lab research group.

My thesis work is centered on ArduSwarm, a micro-UAV testbed for decentralized defensive swarm algorithms. The project integrates Bitcraze Crazyflie hardware, ArduPilot firmware, onboard sensing, and peer-to-peer communication.

## Technical Work

I wrote and integrated ArduPilot HAL drivers for PMW3901 optical flow over SPI and VL53L1x Time-of-Flight ranging over I2C to enable infrastructure-free indoor localization.

I also integrated a multi-processor architecture that routes MAVLink between an STM32 flight controller, ESP32/GAP8 companion computer, Wi-Fi telemetry link, and high-level swarm command interface.

## Results

Indoor experiments achieved 0.353 m 3D localization RMSE against ground truth, with approximately 5 ms communication latency and 333 ms decentralized control latency reported in the CSME 2026 paper.
