## Overview

This project began after a commercial external hard drive failed and caused complete data loss. I wanted to design a more reliable replacement that supported high transfer speeds, at least 1 TB of storage, offline operation, and portable use.

## Design Direction

The selected architecture used a Samsung 980 Pro 1 TB SSD paired with a Trebleet 2-in-1 Thunderbolt/USB enclosure. SSD storage was chosen over a mechanical hard drive for better reliability, shock tolerance, and read/write performance.

## Thermal Analysis

To improve sustained transfer performance, I modeled heat transfer from the SSD to the enclosure and surrounding air. The analysis focused on natural convection and compared possible geometry and material changes.

Potential improvements included:

- Increasing exterior surface area with cooling fins.
- Comparing aluminum and copper enclosure materials.
- Evaluating the thermal effect of different enclosure geometries.

## Outcome

CFD simulations supported the design decisions and helped identify enclosure modifications that could improve cooling performance. The next step was to assemble and test the device against the original constraints in real use.
