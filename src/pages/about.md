Thanks for visiting my portfolio. I am a mechanical engineering MASc candidate at the University of Ottawa with a focus on embedded robotics, micro-UAV systems, mechanical design, and practical engineering analysis.

I like work that connects software, hardware, and physical constraints. My current research is ArduSwarm, an open-source testbed for decentralized drone swarm research using Bitcraze Crazyflie hardware and the ArduPilot flight stack.

Before graduate research, I completed my mechanical engineering co-op degree at the University of Waterloo, worked in building systems consulting, HVAC systems design, asset management engineering, and built several hands-on personal projects.

## Interests

Outside of formal work, I stay busy with electronics, 3D printing, cars, skiing, and small hardware builds. I have built and upgraded PCs and laptops, repaired electronics for friends and family, designed a custom SSD enclosure with CFD-driven thermal analysis, and built a compact Raspberry Pi audio streamer.

## What I Like Building

- Embedded systems that talk to real sensors and actuators.
- Mechanical designs that can actually be manufactured and tested.
- Robotics platforms that expose the messy details between simulation and real operation.
- Documentation that makes complex systems usable by other people.

## Current Focus

I am currently interested in robotics, embedded software, autonomous systems, flight controls, and practical hardware platforms for research and product development.
