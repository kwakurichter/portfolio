Thanks for visiting my portfolio! I am a mechanical engineering MASc candidate at the University of Ottawa with a focus on embedded robotics, micro-UAV systems, mechanical design, and practical engineering analysis.

I like work that connects software, hardware, and physical constraints. My current research is [ArduSwarm](https://github.com/kwakurichter/ArduSwarm), an open-source testbed for decentralized drone swarm research.

Before graduate research, I completed my Mechanical Engineering BASc degree at the University of Waterloo, worked in building systems consulting, HVAC systems design, asset management engineering, and built several hands-on personal projects.

## Interests

Outside of formal work, I like to keep myself busy with my hobbies. This generally involves tinkering on just about anything and everything I can get my hands onto; 3D printing, cars, small hardware builds, as well as skiing, tennis, and traveling. I have built and upgraded PCs and laptops, repaired electronics for friends and family, designed a [custom SSD enclosure with CFD-driven thermal analysis](/projects/ssd_enclosure/), and built a compact [Raspberry Pi audio streamer](https://github.com/kwakurichter/rpi-audio).

## Current Focus

I am currently interested in robotics, embedded software, autonomous systems, flight controls, and practical hardware platforms for research and product development.
